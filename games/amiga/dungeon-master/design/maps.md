# Dungeon Master - floor maps

All fourteen levels as the game holds them in memory, read with `../tools/dungeon.py @saved` (RAM, the
moment of checkpoint `saved`: party on level 2, deeper levels untouched). Coordinates are the game's own
(X east, Y south, per level, the same numbers as the playbook's warps). Legend: `#` wall, `.` corridor,
`O` pit, `<` stairs up, `>` stairs down, `D` door (any state), `T` teleporter (most are invisible), `F` fake or
hidden wall; a letter marks a square whose first thing is a creature group (key under each map; monsters move
once their level is visited) (RAM). Walls carry ornaments, buttons, keyholes and alcoves that the square type
does not show (verified: keyholes at level 2 (0,3) and (2,12), a door button at (1,0)).

How the levels connect: a staircase square leads to the same absolute place one level down or up; absolute
place = level coordinates + the level's offset (RAM: level 1 stairs down (3,15) + (0,0) = level 2 stairs up
(3,1) + (0,14); verified by the warps `DL3`..`DL14`, shots `env_l3_corridor` .. `env_l14_corridor`).
