# Deep Dungeon III - story and goals

## Overview
A young swordsman sets out to become a hero and stops at Dirige **(verified: prologue, shot `prologue_text`)**.
Monsters have come out of the ground because the seal on a stone monument in the underground was broken
**(verified: shot `ev_monument`; the ending explains the princess broke it by mistake)**. Each dungeon has a master:
Zosem in the cave **(verified: shots `ev_zosem_voice`, `boss_zosem`)**, Barzas in the tower **(verified: shot
`boss_tower_master`)**, and the monster in the castle "who made this world" **(ROM text)**. The king is held in the
castle and the princess is under a monster's spell **(verified: shot `ev_king`)**.

## 1. Threads (from the game's text)

| the castle | front entrance blocked by guards asking for a pass; find a back way | verified text + ROM |
| orbs | orb of light (the underground master hates light; "the tower's master has it"), orb of fire | verified text |

## 2. Ending
The princess thanks the party, explains the broken seal, asks them to set the stones and the sword into the
monument; staff roll; "the End" with the monument **(verified by warping to the ending: shots `ending_princess` to
`ending_the_end`)**.
