# Wizardry III: Legacy of Llylgamyn (DOS) - systems (game-design lens)

The index of everything the game lets the player do and every rule that shapes play: one row per feature,
system or mechanic, with the rule in one line, where the details live and which shots show it. Read it to see
what the game is made of; follow the links for numbers. The same shots also appear in
[interface.md](interface.md), which looks at the screens themselves, and in [content.md](content.md), which
shows the variety of what fills the game; overlap is intended.
Notation: [README.md](README.md). Check: `python3 tools/lenses.py dos/wizardry-3`.

Status: `shown` (a shot shows it at work), `described` (known, no shot: say why), `open` (not understood).
Shot ids are those of set `main` (`../shots/main/index.md`, 228 shots, captured 2026-11-19).

## 1. Core loop
As in Wizardry I and II: form a party in the Castle, climb into the maze, fight, map, come back to rest, heal
and shop (verified: same menus). Wizardry III starts from the heroes of the earlier games reborn at level 1
without goods (`inventory_descendant_sheet`), so the early loop is the classic one again. The fork after floor 1
makes the player keep two parties of different alignment and alternate between the good half (floors 2 and 4)
and the evil half (3 and 5); each half ends in a guardian whose crystal opens the summit
(`boss_found_evil`, `boss_found_good`). One character of the right alignment must then join both crystals into
the Neutral Crystal (`inventory_neutral_crystal`), which the statue on floor 6 turns into the Orb of Earithin
(`inventory_orb`); the orb in the Castle is the ending (`ending_congratulations`). The set plays this whole
chain in one line of checkpoints (`gcastle` ... `gafter`, playbook), with two guardian fights ended by a poke in
their last round.

## 3. How the systems feed each other
- Earlier games -> M)ove -> L)egate -> a level-1 party with honors (verified).
- Fights -> E.P. and gold -> levels (Inn) and gear (Boltac's) -> the guardians (verified with veterans).
- Alignment -> which half a party may enter -> which guardian -> which crystal (verified).
- Both crystals -> one non-neutral bearer invokes his own -> Neutral Crystal -> floor 6 statue -> orb -> Castle
  -> stars for six more characters (verified in play, 2026-11-19).
- Status effects -> Temple -> gold (verified: stoning cost 7000 for three members).
