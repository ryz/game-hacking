# World

## Overview
One overworld of 120 by 120 tiles holds a castle, five towns, a ruined town, four caves and the Dragon King's
castle on an island in the middle, in sight of the start but out of reach until the rainbow bridge is made.
**(verified)** [overworld_tantegel](../shots/main/overworld_tantegel.png),
[rainbow_bridge_built](../shots/main/rainbow_bridge_built.png) Places are entered by stepping on their tile and
left by walking off the edge of their map. **(verified)**

## 3. Roofs
Some buildings in towns are covered by a grey roof. Walking under it blacks out the town and shows only the
inside (Cantlin: a garden with a pond and an old man). **(verified)**
[cantlin_roof](../shots/main/cantlin_roof.png)

## 4. The rainbow bridge
Using the Rainbow Drop (Items) on the sand tip east of the Dragon King's island gives "ryz raised the Rainbow
Drop."; the sea flashes yellow, pink and light blue several times and a bridge tile appears on the water to
the west. **(verified)** [rainbow_drop_use](../shots/main/rainbow_drop_use.png),
[rainbow_bridge](../shots/main/rainbow_bridge.png), [rainbow_bridge_built](../shots/main/rainbow_bridge_built.png)

## 5. Poison swamp
The dark tiles around the Dragon King's castle are swamp. A hero in Loto's armour lost no HP stepping on one.
**(RAM)** [charlock_overworld](../shots/main/charlock_overworld.png) Damage without that armour: **(source)**,
not measured.

## 6. The ending
After the Dragon King falls the hero walks back to Tantegel. The king and two rows of trumpeters wait in the
courtyard at the foot of the throne-room stairs; stepping up to them starts the last scene by itself: "O'
Brave ryz! You've fulfilled the Legend!", the king offers his land, "But ryz said: All that I wish to rule is
myself.", Princess Laura asks "Can't I come, ryz?" (Yes / No), the trumpeters raise their horns, then a text
screen ("GREAT!! YOU REGAINED PEACE TO THE WORLD! ...") and the staff roll (character design Akira Toriyama,
music Koichi Sugiyama). **(verified)** [ending_courtyard](../shots/main/ending_courtyard.png),
[ending_king](../shots/main/ending_king.png), [ending_princess](../shots/main/ending_princess.png),
[ending_fanfare](../shots/main/ending_fanfare.png), [ending_message](../shots/main/ending_message.png),
[ending_credits](../shots/main/ending_credits.png) The king in the throne room only says "Hurray for Loto!
Hurray for ryz!" at that point. **(verified)**
