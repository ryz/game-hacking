# Maps: the labyrinth of Daedalus

## Overview
The whole game is one labyrinth, Daedalus, drawn on a single grid of 128 x 64 squares **(RAM: the party
position is one pair of coordinates for every floor; ROM grid decoded, see playbook "Maps")**. The grid is cut
into blocks of 8 x 8 squares (some into 4 x 4), and each block belongs to one floor of one of six **sections**.
A section is a tower of floors joined by stairs and one elevator; each section ends in a tyrant, and the
tyrant's square is a fixed battle **(computed from the ROM tables; every boss verified by warping there)**.
35 floors in all. Every floor is drawn in `maps/` (one PNG per floor, walls and doors from the ROM, special
squares marked) with the full list of special squares in [maps/grids.md](maps/grids.md) (generated by
`../tools/maps.py`).

Coordinates below are the game's own `x,y` (x grows east, y grows south); the warp token in the playbook takes
them in hex (`WARP:floor:section:xx:yy:facing`).

## 4. How the floors join
- Stairs come in pairs: every "up" square names the square it leads to and that square is a "down" square
  leading back **(computed: the up and down lists mirror each other)**. A few stairs change section (section 3 2F <->
  section 4 at (104,11)/(116,23); section 0 1F warp to section 2) **(computed)**.
- The elevator of a section stops on most of its floors; its door needs a key item (the 8F elevator door is
  one of the "You couldn't enter!" squares of round 1) **(verified for the message; key item inferred)**.
