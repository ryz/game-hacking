# Items, shops and spells

## Overview
The hero carries Herbs and Keys by count and up to eight other items, wears one weapon, one armour and one
shield, and learns ten spells by level. **(verified)** [items_window](../shots/main/dragon-quest_nes_jp_inventory_items_window.png),
[stats_sheet](../shots/main/dragon-quest_nes_jp_inventory_stats_sheet.png), [spell_window](../shots/main/dragon-quest_nes_jp_inventory_spell_window.png) Shops and inns are
counters: stand in front of the counter and Talk towards the keeper. **(verified)**
[shop_weapons](../shots/main/dragon-quest_nes_jp_shop_shop_weapons.png)

## 1. The king's gifts
The three chests in the throne room hold 120 Gold, a Torch and a Key; the Key opens the throne-room door
(Door command), which is the only way out. **(verified)** [throne_room_chest](../shots/main/dragon-quest_nes_jp_system_throne_room_chest.png),
[door_command](../shots/main/dragon-quest_nes_jp_system_door_command.png), [throne_room_door](../shots/main/dragon-quest_nes_jp_gameplay_throne_room_door.png)

## 3. Inn of the first town
"Traveler's Inn. Stay for 6G?" Yes: "Good night!", the screen goes black, "Good morning! Did you sleep well?
Return again!"; HP and MP are full (83 and 64 became 149 and 122) and 6 Gold are gone. **(verified)**
[inn_stay](../shots/main/dragon-quest_nes_jp_shop_inn_stay.png), [inn_morning](../shots/main/dragon-quest_nes_jp_system_inn_morning.png)

## 5. Levels
Level 2 comes at 7 experience and adds 7 to Max HP (15 to 22), among other stats. **(verified)**
[battle_levelup](../shots/main/dragon-quest_nes_jp_battle_battle_levelup.png) At level 21 with 34218 experience the king says "next Level
after 2782 experience", so level 22 comes at 37000. **(verified)** route to
[king_password](../shots/main/dragon-quest_nes_jp_save_king_password.png)
