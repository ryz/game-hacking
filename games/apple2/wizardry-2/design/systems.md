# Wizardry II: The Knight of Diamonds - systems (game-design lens)

The index of everything the game lets the player do and every rule that shapes play: one row per feature,
system or mechanic, with the rule in one line, where the details live and which shots show it. Read it to see
what the game is made of; follow the links for numbers. The same shots also appear in
[interface.md](interface.md), which looks at the screens themselves, and in [content.md](content.md), which
shows the variety of what fills the game; overlap is intended.
Notation: [README.md](README.md). Check: `python3 tools/lenses.py apple2/wizardry-2 main`.
Shots are ids of the set `main` (`../shots/main/index.md`); the set `start` (`../shots/start/`) shows the
transfer and the first party in play order.

Status: `shown` (a shot shows it at work), `described` (known, no shot: say why), `open` (not understood).

## 1. Core loop
Move the party over from Wizardry I, form it in the Tavern, rest at the Inn, equip in the camp, go down, fight
groups of monsters for experience and chests, beat a floor's guardian for its piece of the Knight of Diamonds'
armor and the way down, come back up, rest and identify, go deeper **(verified: the routes of every shot below;
the order of the loop is the DOS port's, source)**. Between loops the party gains pieces of armor rather than
levels: the characters arrive at level 13 and need thousands of E.P. for level 14 **(verified: `inn_stables`)**.

## 3. How the systems feed each other
- Guardian beaten -> its piece -> stronger bearer (armor class, regeneration) -> next guardian **(verified for
  the drops; the bearer's gain seen with all five: `inventory_kod_set`)**.
- All five worn by one character -> Gnilda's test -> the staff, and the five are taken back **(verified)**.
- The roster is the gate: a piece owned by any roster character keeps its guardian away **(verified)**.
- Gold from the Wizardry I ending pays Boltac and the Temple; the 100,000 the sage asks is beyond any one
  character of this party **(verified)**.
